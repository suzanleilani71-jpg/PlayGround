HOW TO DESCEND

◎
Strike the lane

Notes fall toward the bright line. Tap anywhere in a lane — or press D F J K — as a note crosses it.

✧
Perfects build shield

Accurate hits charge a shield that soaks enemy strikes. Misses shred your HP and reset your combo.

✦
Hold the tail

Long notes keep dealing damage while your finger stays down. Release whenever — you just stop the sustain.

⚡
Unleash Fever

Perfects fill the FEVER meter. Pop it for double damage and boosted score. It's the run-saver.

◈
Kill before the song ends

If the track finishes with the enemy alive, it finishes you. Clear the floor, choose a resonance, go deeper.

☠
Boss every 5 depths

Denser patterns, faster tempo, more strikes. Endless descent — your best depth is the score.
